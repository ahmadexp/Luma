The precision libraries are statically linked into WebAssembly. Luma's public
source, pinned dependency archives, and Board/scripts/build-wasm.sh permit
rebuilding and relinking this module with modified versions of the libraries.
The WebAssembly module is loaded from wasm/luma.js and wasm/luma.wasm in the
unpacked app. No integrity restriction prevents replacing a rebuilt module.
